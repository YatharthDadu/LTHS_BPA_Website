A Journey Through Time in Chicago

  • From its founding in 1833 to becoming a bustling metropolis, Chicago's history is as rich as it is diverse.
  • Great Chicago Fire (1871): A major event that led to the extensive rebuilding of the city.
  • World's Columbian Exposition (1893): A landmark world fair that showcased Chicago's resurgence.

Fun Facts About Chicago

  • Chicago's nickname "The Windy City" was coined not just for its weather, but also for its long-winded politicians.
  • The first Ferris wheel made its debut in Chicago in 1893.
  • Chicago River is the only river in the world that flows backward, a result of an engineering marvel.
