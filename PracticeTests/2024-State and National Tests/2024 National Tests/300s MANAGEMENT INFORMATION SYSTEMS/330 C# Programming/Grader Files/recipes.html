Authentic Margherita Pizza

Ingredients:

  • Fresh tomatoes
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Pizza dough

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  3. Spread olive oil on the dough, then add sliced tomatoes, mozzarella cheese, and basil leaves.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  6. Remove from oven, slice, and serve hot.
